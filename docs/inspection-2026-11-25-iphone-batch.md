## Battery health: how unknown is handled now

> **Status: applied to the hosted project on 2026-11-26** as migration version
> `20261126081209_unknown_battery_health_staged_units`. Verified live:
> `opening_stock_units.battery_health` is nullable, `inventory_devices.battery_health`
> is still `NOT NULL`, and `record_opening_stock_unit_battery_health` exists.
> **No devices have been staged yet** — see the note at the end of this section.

Before the migration, the existing opening-stock receiving workflow **could not
record a device whose battery health is unknown**, and neither could the legacy
inventory table:

- `public.opening_stock_units.battery_health` is `integer not null` with
  `check (battery_health between 1 and 100)`.
- `public.inventory_devices.battery_health` is likewise `integer not null` with
  `check (battery_health between 1 and 100)`.
- `public.receive_opening_stock_devices` rejects any device whose
  `battery_health` is not an integer in 1–100
  (`'real IMEI and complete passing inspection required'`), and
  `private.is_valid_opening_stock_inspection` additionally requires **all eight**
  inspection attestations to be literally `true` before a unit can be received or
  activated.

So for the nine handsets reading `No Data` there was no way to persist "battery
health unknown" without either fabricating a number (explicitly forbidden) or
changing the schema. `20261126081209_unknown_battery_health_staged_units.sql`
does the latter, narrowly:

- `opening_stock_units.battery_health` may be `null` **only while staged**;
- `inventory_devices.battery_health` remains `NOT NULL`;
- activation refuses an unmeasured unit;
- `record_opening_stock_unit_battery_health` fills the gap once, staged-only, audited;
- all eight inspection attestations stay mandatory.

Registration is still **not** attempted here, because the remaining blockers
below are not code problems.

The eight attestations are also a physical-inspection record — display, cameras,
buttons, connectivity, charging port, IMEI-vs-device, label match, and Activation
Lock cleared. The photos evidence only the last two (the IMEI is read from the
port and the screen reports `Apple ID Lock: Off`). The remaining six must be
performed on the physical handset by a manager before they can honestly be
attested.

## What is still required

1. ~~A decision on the unknown-battery case~~ — **done and applied**: unknown is
   allowed on staged units only (hosted version `20261126081209`).
2. **A physical inspection pass** on all ten handsets to earn the other six
   attestations.
3. **A manager session with `manage_inventory`, `access_manager_workspace`, and
   `approve_inventory_adjustments`** to perform the receiving and later
   activation. An agent session has no `auth.uid()` and cannot call the
   `security definer` RPCs, which correctly reject unauthenticated callers.
4. **A positive approved sale price per unit** at activation time. No price is
   present in the inspection photos and none may be invented.
5. **A re-read of the iPhone 13 Pro serial number** and the two fields listed as
   unreadable above.
